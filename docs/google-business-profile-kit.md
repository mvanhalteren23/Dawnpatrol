## 4. Questions & answers (post the question, then answer it yourself)

1. **Does it sound like a robot?** No. It uses a natural voice and answers in your business name. If a caller asks, it says it's an AI assistant and offers to take a message or transfer them.
2. **Can I keep my business number?** Yes. Forward your current number, or move it to us. Callers dial the number they already know.
3. **How much does it cost?** CA$139 a month plus GST/HST (US$109 for US businesses), with 200 minutes included and no setup fee. Extra minutes are billed in 100-minute blocks.
4. **Is there a contract?** A 3-month minimum, then cancel any time.
5. **Can it book appointments?** Yes, into your Google Calendar, using your hours and rules.
6. **What happens with urgent calls?** You choose: transferred to your cell, or texted to you straight away.
7. **Can I try it first?** Yes. Book a free demo and hear a receptionist built for your own business.
